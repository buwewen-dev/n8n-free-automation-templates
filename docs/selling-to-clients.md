# Selling these to clients

[Back to the main page](../README.md)

These three automations solve problems almost every small business has: leads that go cold, customers who stop coming back, and calls nobody answers. That makes them some of the easiest automations to sell.

## Who buys what

### Lead Follow-up System

Any business where a fast reply wins the deal.

- **Real estate agents.** Buyer and seller inquiries from Zillow, Facebook ads or the website get a reply the same day and three follow-ups after.
- **Law firms.** Consultation requests are followed up until the person books or says no.
- **Home services** (remodeling, roofing, HVAC, solar). Quote requests stop going cold while the owner is on a job site.
- **Agencies and consultants.** Discovery-call leads from forms and LinkedIn.

### Customer Re-engagement

Any business with repeat customers and a visit history.

- **Med spas.** A client who had a facial 30 days ago gets a friendly check-in, an invitation back at 60 days, and a "how can we do better" at 90.
- **Gyms and studios.** Members who stopped showing up get a nudge before they cancel.
- **Salons and barbers.** Regulars who drift away are invited back.
- **Dental clinics.** Patients overdue for a cleaning get a reminder.

### AI Voice Receptionist

Any business that loses money on missed calls.

- **Clinics and dental offices.** Book appointments after hours and during lunch.
- **Med spas.** Answer "do you have anything Friday?" without a front-desk person.
- **Hotels and rentals.** Take booking questions and messages around the clock.
- **Home services.** Capture the job while the owner is on a roof.

## What to charge

These are typical ranges for small and mid-sized businesses. Price on the value to the client, not on your hours.

| Template | One-time setup | Monthly management |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Follow-up System | $500 to $1,500 | $150 to $300 |
| Customer Re-engagement | $500 to $1,500 | $150 to $300 |
| AI Voice Receptionist | $1,500 to $3,000 | $300 to $800, plus call costs |
| All three as a package | $2,500 to $5,000 | $500 to $1,200 |

## What the setup fee should include

- Connecting the client's Gmail, Sheets and Calendar
- Writing the email copy or the voice agent script in the client's tone
- Importing their existing leads or customer list
- A test run with the client watching
- A short handover call or video

## What the monthly fee should include

- Checking the workflow runs every day and fixing anything that breaks
- Updating the copy, offers and follow-up timing
- A monthly report: emails sent, replies, bookings
- Paying for and managing the voice platform minutes (for the receptionist)

## Easy upsells

- Connect the lead form or CRM directly instead of a Google Sheet
- Add SMS or WhatsApp follow-ups
- Send booked calls to the client's CRM
- Add a review request after each visit

## Tips

- **Show, do not tell.** Run the workflow on a test sheet during the sales call and let the client get the email on their own phone.
- **Start with one.** Sell the template that solves the client's most painful problem, then add the others.
- **Keep it in their accounts.** Build on the client's own Google account and n8n, so they own their data.
